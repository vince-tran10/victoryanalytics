How to play it

  • Identity Block their main attack, hit back super-effectively: Flash Fire Chandelure vs Amber's all-Fire team, Dry Skin Heliolisk vs Manaphy / Beach Water, Levitate Vikavolt + Ground Whiscash vs Factory Electric. Speed control: Sticky Web, Glare; Will-O-Wisp vs physical; Fake Out tempo.
  • Party order = the plan, same in singles and doubles: 1 Vikavolt · 2 Chandelure · 3 Ludicolo · 4 Whiscash · 5 Crabominable · 6 Heliolisk
  • Lead Vikavolt: survives Manaphy, Raichu and Camerupt; Thunderbolt ~1 Manaphy bar a hit. Doubles (Holly): Chandelure beside it for the Bug/Steels
  • Mid Ludicolo Fake Outs on entry (doubles: Surf hits your partner too, fine next to Heliolisk, whose Dry Skin heals from it; never next to Chandelure); Whiscash takes every Electric hit (never Earthquake at the Factory: it turns the field into Short-Circuit); Crabominable hits Rotom, Oricorio, Bellossom
  • Close Heliolisk: fastest member, Glare the Speed Boost users (Scolipede, Blaziken), immune to Water
  • Fields Beach: Surf 1.5x, Mud Barrage 2x, Aura Sphere 1.3x · Factory: Electric 1.2x · Concert: hype rolls, sound 1.5x
