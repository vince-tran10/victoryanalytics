How to play it

  • Identity Slow, hard-hitting core (Rhyperior, Armarouge, Vikavolt) backed by speed control: Musharna's Glare, Vikavolt's Sticky Web, Ludicolo's Fake Out. Annihilape is the fast cleaner.
  • Party order = the plan, same in singles and doubles: 1 Musharna · 2 Rhyperior · 3 Vikavolt · 4 Armarouge · 5 Ludicolo · 6 Annihilape
  • Lead Musharna (survives every opposing lead in all 7 fights): Glare the fastest threat, or hit it. Doubles: Rhyperior leads beside it (survives all 4 doubles leads)
  • Mid Rhyperior and Armarouge do the damage on slowed foes (Armarouge Calm Minds when safe); Vikavolt sets Sticky Web on entry; Ludicolo Fake Outs the turn it comes in
  • Close Annihilape: fastest member, Sucker Punch priority, Rage Fist strongest late after taking hits
  • Watch Manaphy one-shots Rhyperior: keep it in the back there. Speed Boost users: Protect turn 1, attack turn 2
