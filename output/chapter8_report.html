How to play it

  • Identity Balance / tempo core of 4 (stays all Chapter 9): Intimidate Arcanine-Hisui leads, Golisopod-Aevian is the win condition (Skill Link + Swords Dance), Gholdengo is status-immune with Thunder Wave speed control, Vaporeon is the bulky Haze answer to setup. No shared weakness. The last 2 slots are swaps per fight: now Copperajah + Muk-Alola for Dark Gardevoir (Muk-Alola resists its Dark Pulse).
  • Party order = the plan, same in singles and doubles: 1 Arcanine-Hisui · 2 Golisopod-Aevian · 3 Gholdengo · 4 Vaporeon · 5 Copperajah · 6 Muk-Alola
  • Lead Arcanine-Hisui: Intimidate on entry, Extreme Speed picks off low foes. Doubles: Golisopod-A beside it (Intimidate covers its Swords Dance turn)
  • Mid Gholdengo Thunder Waves the fastest threat (status moves can't touch it); Vaporeon Hazes any setup (Calm Mind, Dragon Dance) and walls Water / Fire / Ice
  • Close Swap slots: Copperajah (Steel hits Fairy) + Muk-Alola (resists Dark, Clear Smog) for Dark Gardevoir. After it, swap in the next fight's counters
  • Dark Gardevoir move Copperajah to slot 1 for that fight (it leads vs Gardevoir; Fights tab has the plan)
