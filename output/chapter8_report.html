Verdict singles vs doubles

Your party is excellent in singles and good but beatable in doubles.

In singles nothing in your boxes does meaningfully better: the best six I found is within about 1%. In doubles the best six is about 6% stronger, and five of Chapter 8's biggest fights are doubles: Texen, Crawli, Eli & Sharon, Geara & Zetta and Rift Giratina.

The gap comes from three things. Whiscash's Earthquake hits your own partner. Lapras-Aevian and Leavanny-Aevian are frail to the Rock, Fire and Flying moves that Mountain and Volcanic Top boost by 1.5x. And nobody has Fake Out or redirection to protect Golisopod while it sets up.

Your instincts were right: Golisopod-Aevian is the best mon you own, and the crest really does make Whiscash weakness-free.

Community, crests and shadow Pokémon

What players rate

  • A Reborn Evolved poster ranked Aevian Golisopod the best Aevian, ahead of Aevian Gyarados, Breloom and Milotic (forum).
  • "Strongest Pokémon" threads name Crest Delcatty, Crest Bastiodon and Haxorus (thread). Those crests are Chapter 14+ or Karma Files, so they're not available yet.
  • Older threads like Wide Guard Fighting types such as Hariyama and Machamp for doubles, and Thick Fat Mamoswine (thread).
  • Most community discussion predates V14, so treat it as background. The matchup numbers in this report are from the current game data.

Crests you can get this chapter

  • Zoroark Crest: Garufa Sanctuary, behind the Hisuian Zorua block puzzle. Zoroark gains the copied mon's ability and STAB. You have Zoroark in Active.
  • Electrode Crest: Route 6, centre chest of the Xen battleship nearest Valor Mountain. It halves the target's Defense and adds stats based on Speed. You own Hisuian Electrode.
  • Phione Crest: Tale of Two Hearts quest in Kristiline.
  • Coming in Chapter 9: Meganium Crest, which gives your side damage reduction and healing every turn. Your shiny Lv 25 Chikorita is worth raising for it.

Source: Rejuvenation wiki, Crests.

Shadow Pokémon to snag this chapter

  • Shadow Tatsugiri (Professor Jenner, Valor Summit): Lv 51 with 31 IVs, Nasty Plot, Dragon Pulse, Ice Beam, Flip Turn. The best of the three.
  • Shadow Drifblim (Analyst Mephisto, Route 6): purifies with Hurricane and Mystical Fire, both 1.5x on Mountain and Volcanic Top.
  • Shadow Purugly (Analyst Zena, Route 6): Swords Dance and Earthquake.

Source: Rejuvenation wiki, Shadow Pokémon.
