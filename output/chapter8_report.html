Game plan

  • Built to last, not around a field. Picked for the fights you have left (Aelita and Narcissa are done), singles counted double, Protect in every 4th slot, and scored both with and without field effects. No attacking type hits more than 2 of the six super-effectively: Flying, Ghost, Ice and Water hit two each, everything else hits one or none.
  • Two win conditions. Golisopod hits hardest: in singles it one-shots 31 of 44 boss mons, 37 after one Swords Dance. Annihilape is the safe opener: only 2 of 44 can one-shot it, and Rage Fist grows every time it's hit.
  • Lead Annihilape takes the first hit, then Rage Fist. Chandelure leads into physical teams and burns them with Will-O-Wisp; keep its Focus Sash, it's the frailest of the six.
  • Mid Azumarill (Huge Power) handles Dragons, Dark and Fire. Whiscash's crest removes every weakness, so it can switch into almost anything and hit back with Earthquake and Aqua Tail.
  • Closer Golisopod uses Swords Dance only when the foe can't hurt it, then sweeps. Glalie-Aevian's Grassy Surge heals each turn, and Grassy Glide hits first.
  • Only the lead changes, never the six. Golisopod leads vs Tommen, Troye & Helen, Giratina and the ??? Factory fight. Whiscash leads vs both Ancient Guards and Zena. Azumarill leads vs Neved and Amber. Glalie-Aevian leads vs Eli & Sharon, Kyogre and Manaphy. Annihilape leads vs Jenner and Geara & Zetta. Chandelure leads vs Groudon (burn it first), Crawli, Danni & Layla, Holly & Rorim and Marcie. Texen (bring 3): Chandelure, Annihilape, Golisopod.

Checked against the fights you have left (103 boss mons). Without a one-shot answer: the three bosses with boosted HP (Groudon, Kyogre, Manaphy), plus a few that take 67–99% (Kleavor, Gyarados, Dragonite, Skeledirge). Most often one-shot on your side: Chandelure (19 boss hits), Azumarill (5 boss hits), Golisopod-Aevian (4 boss hits). Keep Chandelure's Focus Sash on and switch it out of bad matchups early.

Hardest fight: Kyogre (Lv 60 boss in rain). Nobody one-shots it: lead Glalie-Aevian and chip it with Grass moves. For Groudon, Exeggutor (Chlorophyll doubles its Speed in sun) is the one swap worth making.
