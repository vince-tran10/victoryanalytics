Game plan

  • Lead Annihilape + Gholdengo for fast damage. Remove Typhlosion before Eruption fires; Sucker Punch finishes weakened fast foes first.
  • Mid Rhyperior handles Fire and fliers. Glaceon's Icy Wind slows the back line.
  • Closer Leavanny uses Rage Powder, Golisopod uses Swords Dance, then sweeps. In singles, Golisopod sets up on a switch-in it walls.
  • Only the lead changes, never the six. Eli & Sharon: Rhyperior + Gholdengo lead. Groudon: Glaceon leads and Gholdengo sits out. Giratina: lead the closer pair. Amber: Rhyperior leads. Texen (bring 3): Gholdengo, Annihilape, Golisopod.

Team strength, in plain terms

Boss mons someone in your six can one-shot66/68
…and does it before that foe moves37/68
After one Golisopod Swords Dance60/68

The only fights without a one-shot answer are Groudon (a boss with boosted HP: chip it with Glaceon's Ice moves) and Eli & Sharon's Milotic (best hit: Annihilape, about 85%). Most often one-shot on your side: Leavanny-Aevian (12 boss hits), Glaceon (7 boss hits), Rhyperior (7 boss hits). Leavanny's Focus Sash is what lets it live through those.
