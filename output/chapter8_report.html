Game plan

  • Lead Annihilape + Gholdengo for fast damage. Remove Typhlosion before Eruption fires.
  • Mid Rhyperior handles Fire and fliers. Glaceon's Icy Wind slows the back line.
  • Closer Leavanny uses Rage Powder, Golisopod uses Swords Dance, then sweeps. In singles, Golisopod sets up on a switch-in it walls.
  • Only the lead changes, never the six. Eli & Sharon: Rhyperior + Gholdengo lead. Groudon: Glaceon leads and Gholdengo sits out. Giratina: lead the closer pair. Amber: Rhyperior leads. Texen (bring 3): Gholdengo, Annihilape, Golisopod.
